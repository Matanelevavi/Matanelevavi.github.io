Agents (personal)

A private, single-user assistant. Not a public service.

What it is

Agents (personal) is a personal software project by Matanel Levavi. It is an AI assistant that its owner uses through a private Telegram chat to stay organized. It is built for one person only, its owner, and it is not offered to the public or to other users.

What it does with Google data

After the owner explicitly grants access to his own Google accounts, the assistant can:

  • read calendar events and create or update events in the owner's calendar;
  • read email messages and prepare email drafts for the owner to review (the assistant is designed not to send email on its own);
  • read and manage the owner's tasks;
  • read the names and metadata of files in Google Drive.

These capabilities are used to produce daily briefings, to find free time, to summarize and draft replies, and to keep a task list up to date.
